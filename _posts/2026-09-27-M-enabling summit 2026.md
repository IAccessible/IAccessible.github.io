I’ll be at the [M-Enabling Summit 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/showcase/m-enabling-summit/posts/?feedView=all) in D.C. next week.
A special shout-out to [Damian Sian](https://www.linkedin.com/in/damiansian/?isSelfProfile=false) from Adobe, who will lead a design accessibility workshop on 10/7. The session draws on [IAccessible’s](https://www.linkedin.com/company/79507572/admin/dashboard/) pioneering work with Adobe’s Product Equity team to bring lived disability experience directly into the design process across Adobe’s products.
Accessibility, like nearly every field, is abuzz with ideas sparked by AI. The big question: how will it transform equitable access for people with disabilities?
So far, the gains have been more exploratory and incremental than radically transformative.
That’s why conferences like this matter: they give us a place to compare notes, test ideas, and make sense of a fast-changing landscape.
I’m looking forward to meeting the experts and organizations doing this work and sharing how [IAccessible](https://www.linkedin.com/company/79507572/admin/dashboard/) is keeping the lived experience of people with disabilities front and center.
