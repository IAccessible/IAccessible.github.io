*Last updated: September 19, 2026*

This privacy note applies only to the IAccessible customer feedback survey.

## What the survey collects

The survey collects your answers about:

- Why your organization selected IAccessible.
- The value and impact of IAccessible's work.
- The IAccessible services your organization used.
- Any optional testimonial you choose to provide.
- Your choices about testimonial use, follow-up, case studies, and serving as a customer reference.

The survey does not require you to sign in and does not automatically collect your name or email address. Your organization, name, and work email are optional.

Please do not include confidential, sensitive, classified, procurement-restricted, or personally identifiable information in your survey answers.

## How IAccessible uses responses

IAccessible may use survey responses to:

- Understand why customers select IAccessible.
- Improve our services and customer experience.
- Analyze and report combined statistics about the impact of our work.
- Develop marketing materials and case studies.
- Contact you only when you provide contact information and indicate that follow-up is permitted.

IAccessible will use testimonials and identifying organization information according to the permission you select in the survey. If you select an option that requires written confirmation, IAccessible will seek that confirmation before identifying your organization.

## Where responses are stored

The survey is hosted in a group-owned Microsoft Form. Survey responses are stored through Microsoft 365 and may be accessed by authorized members of the IAccessible team for the purposes described above.

Microsoft may process technical and service information when you load or submit the form. For information about Microsoft's practices, see the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Tracking on the survey page

The IAccessible customer feedback page does not load IAccessible's Google Analytics, HubSpot, or Instantly tracking scripts. The embedded Microsoft Form may use technologies required to provide the form.

## Questions

For questions about this survey or this privacy note, email [info@iaccessible.net](mailto:info@iaccessible.net).
