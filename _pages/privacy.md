*Last updated: September 19, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how IAccessible collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, contact us, or submit information through a form linked from this website.

## Information we collect

We may collect:

- Information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, and the contents of messages or requests.
- Information submitted through surveys and service-request forms, including feedback about our services, optional testimonials, and your preferences about follow-up or public use.
- Technical and usage information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and interactions with the website. Most pages may use Google Analytics, HubSpot, and Instantly for analytics and related website services.

The customer feedback page does not load IAccessible's Google Analytics, HubSpot, or Instantly tracking scripts. The embedded survey is provided by Microsoft Forms, which may process technical information under Microsoft's own privacy terms.

## How we use information

We may use information to:

- Respond to questions, requests, and inquiries.
- Provide and improve our services, website, and customer experience.
- Analyze feedback and report aggregated results.
- Contact people who ask us to follow up about a response, case study, or customer reference.
- Use testimonials or organization information only according to the permission selected by the respondent and any required written confirmation.
- Maintain the security and operation of our website and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## How we share information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, forms, communications, analytics, and business services. These providers may include Microsoft, GitHub, Google, HubSpot, and Instantly. They process information according to their agreements with us and their own privacy terms.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of IAccessible, our customers, or others.

We do not publish customer feedback, testimonials, personal names, or organization names unless the respondent has provided the applicable permission.

## Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, and to meet legal, accounting, or operational requirements.

## Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. You may also contact us to ask about information you have provided or to request that we correct or delete it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

You can manage cookies and similar technologies through your browser settings. Blocking them may affect some website features.

## Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Third-party services and links

Our website may contain embedded content or links to services operated by other organizations. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the date above.

## Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email [info@iaccessible.net](mailto:info@iaccessible.net).
